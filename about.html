About ScanGuide

ScanGuide is a fictional brand created for a front-end portfolio demo. It shows how a printed QR code on a product can lead a person to a fast, mobile-friendly page with a tutorial video in their own language and a simple way to share their location.

What this demo covers

  • Permanent short QR addresses that can be re-pointed without reprinting.
  • One reusable scan page driven by a single content file.
  • Six interface languages with automatic detection.
  • Location sharing that stays inside the browser and the messaging app.

Demo project. The products, videos and brand are fictional or placeholders and are not affiliated with any real company.
